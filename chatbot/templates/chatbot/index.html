IECMS Companion

IECMS Companion explains Zimbabwean legal terms and general court procedure, and shows you how to use the Integrated Electronic Case Management System (IECMS) using its approved user guide.

  • Legal terms and procedure — plain-language explanations grounded in approved legal sources.
  • Using IECMS — documented steps for accounts, login, password recovery, filing a case, adding parties, signing and merging documents, submitting a case, payments, My Workspace, case records, notifications and the calendar.
  • Traceable answers — citations show the source title and, for the manual, the PDF page number.
  • Document summaries — upload a PDF, Word, text, Markdown or HTML file for a plain-language summary. A summary is generated from your file, not from the approved sources.
  • Follow-up questions — continue from the previous answer with “Explain the next step” or “What does this option mean?”

This tool explains general information only. It does not advise you on your own case. It cannot access your IECMS account, case records, notifications or calendar, and it cannot file, sign, merge, submit or pay for anything for you.

IECMS guidance is based on the IECMS Getting Started Guide — Public Users, Version 1.1 (2021). The live interface may have changed, so confirm the current steps in IECMS.
