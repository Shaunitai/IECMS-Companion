## Application for Condonation

"Condonation" refers to a court's power to excuse a party's failure to comply
with a rule or a time limit. An application for condonation is a request asking
the court to overlook that non-compliance so that the matter may proceed.

General practice materials describe such an application as ordinarily needing to
address:

- what the rule or time limit actually required;
- how and why the applicant failed to comply with it;
- the applicant's prospects of success if the indulgence is granted; and
- any prejudice that the other party would suffer if it is granted.

The expression is also used more broadly for any request that a court excuse a
procedural defect. Because the requirements differ between courts and between
instruments, a general description such as this one cannot state what a
particular court would require.

This description is generic and jurisdiction-neutral. It is not a statement of
Zimbabwean law.
